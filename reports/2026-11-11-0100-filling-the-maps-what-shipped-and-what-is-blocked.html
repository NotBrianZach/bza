The finding that changes the plan

isTeachable means "has at least one knowledge point", and every surface in the app reported it as the number of concepts that "have problems written for them". Those are two different claims and for three concepts they disagree:

  • logarithm-definition — 2 knowledge points, 0 tasks
  • logarithm-rules — 2 knowledge points, 0 tasks
  • solving-exponential-equations — 1 knowledge point, 0 tasks

They were counted as practisable while offering nothing a learner could be set. Found by looking for the next concept to author and discovering three that already claimed to be done.

The pattern is not random, and that is the load-bearing part. Those three are exactly the concepts whose natural task is solving an equation or stating a definition. TASK_KINDS has five members and the two that would serve them are the two that are not verifiable:

The linear-equations question, answered

The previous report flagged a check it had not done:

Needs a check I have not done: whether a "solve for x" answer has a NamedForm the verifier can check, or whether it has to be posed as an evaluate on the root. That check gates the estimate.

Checked. There is no verifiable "solve" kind. The answer to 3x + 5 = 14 is a solution — not a transformed expression and not an evaluation — and the expression language parses expressions, not equations. Authoring linear-equations now would produce a fourth concept with material and no problems and make the overstatement above worse. It was not authored, deliberately.

What shipped

fraction-arithmetic — and a correction

Authored into arithmetic-foundations: three knowledge points, three evaluate tasks with integer symbols. It was the hole under the whole algebra syllabus — combining-rational was reachable without ever having added ½ + ⅓.

The previous report framed this as a decision about accepting a new gate. It was subtler than that, and the distinction is worth keeping: authoring the concept did not add a gate, it made an existing edge bite. fraction-arithmetic → combining-rational was already in ALGEBRA_FOUNDATIONS.links; gatingPrerequisitesOf looked straight through it because the endpoint had no material. A duplicate link added during authoring was removed, and the atlas assertion that the concept must not appear in the chain was flipped with the reason recorded.

Two drafts were caught by the task validator, both real faults rather than style:

  1. transform_to_form would have made arithmetic — the root of the map — depend on the CAS worker, so an outage would wall off the entry point rather than a branch. The subject's contract is that every task is settled locally, asserted over all of them.
  2. The division task was a free mark: single_fraction means "one quotient, no top-level sum", and (2x/3)/(4/5) already is one, so the given satisfied the form it was asking for.

Four journeys, and a map instead of a list

Ten journeys now, 3 to 16 steps. The journey list became a journey map — a third position on the Concepts scope switch, with two derived relations: stage (one journey's destination lies on another's route, so finishing it is literal progress) and shares (overlap with no containment). Current shape: 13 stage edges, 4 shared-ground edges, 1 island.

The shares relation discounts concepts common to more than half the journeys, or every route's shared arithmetic base connects all of them and the drawing carries no information.

Three concept-map nodes

Parametric curves, transformations of the plane, loci and regions — into coordinate-geometry, the thinnest area at seven nodes. Polar coordinates was the obvious fourth and already existed in trigonometry; the duplicate test caught it, and also caught congruence-and-similarity, an id invented for something filed as two concepts.

The plan from here

1. Unblock the practice map (engine work, not authoring)

This is the only item that changes the ceiling. Two independent routes, and the first is cheaper:

  • Give the three read-only concepts real tasks. log is in ALLOWED_FUNCTIONS, so the worker can rule equivalence. It needs either a new NamedForm for "logs expanded into a sum" — the type forces a checker in FORM_CHECKS, which is the actual work — or a worker-ruled evaluate path, since exactValue returns null for function nodes. Closes a known 3-concept gap and unblocks the exponents subject.
  • A verifiable solve kind. Larger: the expression language parses expressions, so equations would need representing, and a solution set is not a single value (no solutions, one, infinitely many). This is what linear-equations, linear-inequalities, systems-of-linear-equations and absolute-value-equations are all waiting on — four mapped nodes that a reader would expect to be practisable before most of what is.

2. Keep extending the narratives map — but it is near its bound

Ten journeys over 35 teachable concepts. The bound is real: a journey must end on a teachable concept, so the narratives map cannot outgrow the practice map. Remaining unused destinations with a genuine arc: polynomial-roots, partial-fractions-quadratic, function-composition, determinant, plane-vectors. Perhaps four or five more before they start overlapping each other more than they illuminate.

3. Concept map — extend when a reader would notice a hole

Still cheap and still gates nothing. But the ratio argument from the previous report stands and has worsened with the real figure known: 294 nodes against 32 practisable is better than 9:1, and every honest surface in the app has to print it. Add nodes where a reader would notice their absence; do not bulk-author to make the atlas look fuller.

The authoring checklist, as it actually played out

The move procedure from the previous report is correct and was followed. Two additions from doing it:

  1. Delete the mapped node from its area.
  2. Add the authored concept, with knowledge points and tasks — isTeachable is satisfied by knowledge points alone, which is how three concepts ended up readable and not practisable.
  3. Restate every incoming edge in the area's links. The symptom of forgetting is not an error; it is a syllabus quietly becoming a root of the map.
  4. Check whether an edge into the new concept already exists. If it does, authoring makes it bite immediately and there is no concept-then-edge sequencing available. This is what fraction-arithmetic did.
  5. Update the pinned counts. Several tests pin subject sizes (5 concepts, 15 knowledge points, 15 tasks, 4 blocked). They are meant to be updated, and they are what caught each mistake above.

What I would still not do

  • Author a concept whose tasks cannot be settled exactly. Unchanged, and now with three worked examples of what it costs — they are sitting in the exponents subject as material nobody can practise.
  • Let isTeachable absorb the distinction. It gates introduction and must keep meaning "there is material here", which is the right question for the scheduler. "Can they go and do it" is a different question and now has its own function.
  • Bulk-author mapped nodes. See the ratio above.
